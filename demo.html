What SteamFox does that day

    Where the data comes from

    1. Spot prices: Pörssisähkö.net free API, Finnish prices in c/kWh with VAT: api.porssisahko.net/v1/latest-prices.json. Tomorrow's prices appear each afternoon after the auction.
    2. Backup price sources: Sähkötin (api.sahkotin.fi/prices) and the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (Finland zone 10YFI-1--------U, free token).
    3. Market source: Nord Pool day-ahead auction, where the prices are set.
    4. Transfer fees: the sauna's network operator, such as Oulun Energia, Caruna, Elenia or Helen Sähköverkko. The live service stores each operator's published price list.
    5. History and testing: Fingrid open data for past prices and grid reserve markets.

    In the live service, the SteamFox server fetches these every day and plans the heater. This demo has two real days built in, fetched from Pörssisähkö.net on 9 October 2026.

    Load the latest prices

    This page can't contact other websites itself, so you can paste the data in:

    1. Open the Pörssisähkö.net price feed.
    2. Select all the text and copy it.
    3. Paste it below and press Load prices.

    Spot prices include 25.5% VAT, as published; VAT-registered businesses reclaim the VAT (tick "Show costs without VAT"). Site types, heater sizes, opening hours and visitor numbers are examples, not real customers. Heater cycling is simplified: about 60% average power while open after warm-up, 35% to hold heat before opening, 40% in eco mode. The live service controls the heater through its existing contactor, installed by an electrician.
