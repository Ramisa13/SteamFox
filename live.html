Where the saving comes from

    SteamFox drops to eco standby when the sauna is quiet and power is expensive, and moves the morning warm-up to the cheapest slot.

    Model: heater runs at 100% while warming up, about 60% on average while open, 40% in eco standby and 35% to hold heat before opening. Prices are spot prices incl. VAT; transfer fees and electricity tax are not included, so real euro savings are a little higher. Visitor patterns are examples until pilot sensors are installed.
